Trips: Rafting Kayaking Camping

White-water rafting is our most well known activity. The river rapids take you doen the Aspen river with different levels of raid. Therefore, we can accomodate from beginners to rafting experts. There are many packages to choose from including half day rafting trips, full day rafting trips, and rafting in the moonlight during waxing gibbous and full moon. In addition to the thrill, enjoy breathtaking scenic views along the Aspen river, watch birds or go for a short hike at the end of the route. Our rafters raft in groups of 6.

We cater for those who who want a peronal experience with the water. Come and enjoy a solo or dual kayak experience. Kayak in the easier rapid parts of the river. Our kayak package includes kayaking with an instructor when needed. The packages offer daytime and moonlight kayaking during waxing gibbous and full moon. This is a great package for those who want to relax on the water. The kayaking side of the river offers astounding vies of the Canopy mountains.

Camp in the forest and watch the stars light up the sky at night. Our riverside camping offers breathtaking sunrises and sunsets. The river by the campside is great for swimming and children friendly. breakfast and dinner are included in the package. Our camping package includes camping gear.
